Week 2, what it turned on

Calvin played a perfect lineup and lost. No Suh For You scored 109.18 of a possible 109.18, the only flawless week in the league, and went down to The Fosterbas by 6.38.

August West fell from 185.66 to 72.22, a 113.44 collapse and the worst managed week of the season at 65.9%. Kyle Pitts started again for 2.00 while Dalton Schultz put up 20.00 on the bench. Two weeks, two Pitts starts, 2.00 points between them.

Kozel benched Patrick Mahomes for the second week running. Mahomes scored 29.98 in street clothes while Lamar Jackson started for 15.80. He did the same thing in week 1. Antonio Banderas is 0–2 and last in points scored.

Evan is not the problem. His roster is. Saucy Move evan scored 59.06, the lowest total of the season, from a 97.4% efficient lineup with 3.10 points on the bench. There was nothing else to start.

Alex is the best manager in the league and has one win. 98.9% efficiency, the fewest points benched, the most points scored, and a .500 record.

Things worth knowing

Sam is the only three-peat in league history. 2018, 2019 and 2020, and he did it without leading the league in scoring in any of them. In 2019 he won the title with the fifth-most points, 329.86 behind Zac. Then came 10th, 4th, 10th, 8th and 11th. Nobody else has swung that far in either direction.

Foster has nine podiums in twelve seasons and the best average finish in the league at 3.50. He also owns both ends of the single-week record: 211.90 in week 16 of 2021, the highest anyone has scored, and 43.10 in week 17 of 2022, the lowest. Fifty-three weeks apart.

Kozel has used the same team name for twelve straight years and finished last in four of them, including three in a row from 2018 to 2020. Worst average finish in the league, no titles, one podium. He does hold the longest win streak on record, thirteen games in 2014, a season he finished fourth.

Austin has never finished in the top three. Twelve seasons, nine different team names, three last-place finishes, and the longest losing streak in league history at nine games in 2022. He is also on the wrong end of the largest beatdown ever recorded, Foster’s 118.52-point win in 2021, back when he was Judge Jeudy.

Brooks is the best manager never to win. Average finish of 4.75, four podiums, and the third-highest season total anyone has posted, 1,930.72 in 2018, which got him third place.

chrisk won the first season on record and vanished. 2014 champion as Taste my rainbow, gone by 2015, replaced by Alex. He is the only departed manager in the archive and retains a perfect record: one season, one title.

Cook is the reigning volatility king. Three titles in four years from 2021, the highest season total ever recorded at 2,009.24 in 2022, and eight team names across twelve years.
